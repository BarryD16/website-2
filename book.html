Our promise

Fees that don't break the bank. And a promise to match.

I've run small businesses, so I know how tight cashflow can be. Everything we do is priced for a small business, not a corporate budget, and it comes with a simple promise: the work we do should save you money, not cost you money. If it doesn't, we won't charge you for it.

We agree up front what "saving" looks like for your business, in time or in money, so there's never any argument about it later.

Common questions

Honest answers, before you book

Why can't I book for tomorrow?

Bookings open from two days ahead so I can fit you around my other appointments and give you my full attention. If it's urgent, send a message or ring 07824 116493 and I'll do my best.

What should I have ready?

Nothing to prepare. Just have a think about the two jobs in your week you'd most like to never do again, and the decisions that keep you thinking about work after hours.

Can I get help paying for it?

Possibly. Depending on where you're based, regional business support schemes can sometimes help cover the cost of AI and digital help. Ask on the call and I'll point you to what's available.
