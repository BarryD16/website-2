Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you decide what to do next

These FAQs are written in the same plain-English style your clients are likely to search for.

Do small businesses really need AI governance?

Usually yes, even if it is light-touch. A simple framework often prevents confusion and helps teams use tools more consistently.

Can you help us write an internal AI policy?

Yes. Policy support can be practical and proportionate, built around your workflows, risk tolerance and team needs.

Is this separate from AI training?

It can be delivered separately, but it often works best alongside training so expectations and good habits are reinforced together.
