How changes work

Four steps, all of them yours

No tickets, no waiting, no "the web person is on holiday".

  • 1. Make the change. Type it into the editor, or ask AI: "change our Saturday hours to 9 till 1"
  • 2. Check the preview. A private link showing exactly how it will look
  • 3. Publish. It's live within a minute or two
  • 4. Change your mind? Every version is kept, so you can put the old one back

It's exactly how this website is run.

Common questions

Honest answers, before you book

Plain English answers to the questions we get asked most often.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It sits on your own domain, and we set things up so you're never stuck relying on us to make a change. That's the whole point.

What if I'm not techy at all?

That's exactly who it's built for. If you can write an email, you can update your site. And if you'd rather just say what you want changed, AI can do it for you and show you a preview first.

What does it cost to keep running?

We'll tell you the running costs up front, before we start. For most small business sites they're low.

Fed up with a website you can't change?
