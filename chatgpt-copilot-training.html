Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you decide what to do next

These FAQs are written in the same plain-English style your clients are likely to search for.

Should we train on ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude and Gemini together?

Often yes. Many teams benefit from understanding where each tool is useful, how they differ, and which use cases fit your business best.

Can this be delivered to managers and frontline staff separately?

Yes. Training can be split by role so each group sees the workflows, risks and examples most relevant to them.

Do we need clear guidance before using these tools?

Not always before first exploration, but having clear guidance early usually reduces confusion and risk.
