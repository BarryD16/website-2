How it works

An hour, in your venue, with your people

We come to wherever you meet. Church hall, community centre, library, school. Bring twenty questions or one. We'll answer them honestly.

  • Suitable for any sized group, from ten people to a hundred
  • One hour by default, longer if you want a bit more depth
  • Genuinely free, no catch, no follow-up sales call
  • We just want more people round here to feel confident about AI
Common questions

Honest answers, before you book

Plain English answers to the questions we get asked most often.

Who are these sessions for?

Local groups, charities, residents' associations, school PTAs, U3A groups, faith groups, hobby clubs. Anyone with members or volunteers who'd benefit from a friendly chat about AI.

Are they really free?

Yes, genuinely. No "free introduction" followed by a sales pitch. Round here matters to us, and helping more people feel confident about AI is part of how we want to work.

Do people need to know anything about tech?

Not a thing. The whole point is that it's friendly, clear and jargon-free. If anyone in your group can use a smartphone, they can keep up.

Would a free session work for your group?

Drop us a line. Tell us about your group, where you meet, how many people. We'll come back with what we could put together for you. No agenda, no pitch.
