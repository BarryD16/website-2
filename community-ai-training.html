Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you decide what to do next

These FAQs are written in the same plain-English style your clients are likely to search for.

Who are these sessions for?

They are well suited to local groups, charities, community organisations and other local audiences who would benefit from a practical introduction to AI.

Are the sessions really free?

Yes. These introductory community sessions are offered free of charge as part of our local commitment.

Do people need technical knowledge to join?

No. Sessions are designed to be clear, friendly and jargon-free, with practical examples that help people feel more confident.

Would a free session be useful for your group?

Get in touch and we can talk about your group, your audience and whether a free introductory session would be a good fit.
