Practical AI help for small businesses in County Durham

Round here

In person, across County Durham

From Durham City to the Dales, County Durham is full of small businesses where one or two people do everything. That's exactly who we work with.

Where we work: Durham, Bishop Auckland, Newton Aycliffe, Spennymoor, Barnard Castle, Chester-le-Street, Consett, Seaham, Peterlee, and the villages of Teesdale and Weardale.

Much of the county is rural, and a missed call or a slow reply can mean a lost job to someone closer. AI that answers, follows up and keeps on top of the admin helps a small rural business hold its own against bigger firms.

It isn't just the hours AI gives back. It's the hundred small decisions a day (who to chase, what to reply, what to post) that it can take off your plate, so you save your best thinking for the work that matters.

Sole traders, family firms and small teams up to about twenty people: trades, cafés, salons, shops, professional services and more. If one or two people do most of the admin, we can probably help.

Our fees are set for small businesses, not corporate budgets, and the work should save you money, not cost you money. If it doesn't, we won't charge you for it.
